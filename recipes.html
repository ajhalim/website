Divorced Dad InstaPot Chicken

2-3lbs of chicken thighs 1 onion( suggest yellow/sweet, typically cheapest and you won’t really notice unique onion flavors) 2-3Yukon/Gold potatoes 2 beefsteak tomatoes(The big ones that look kinda like apples) 2 bell peppers(anycolor, I personally like greens. Cheapest and in this case a unique color) 3+ cloves garlic(add more as you see fit) ==(optional)== 1-2 cans beans[black/chickpea](optional. Adding this to make it a little more filling) 1-2 cups of broth(can use better than bouillon. Ratio being 1 tsp paste and 1 cup water to make 1 cup broth) Spices: 2.5 tsp salt 1.5tsp garlic powder 1.5tsp onion powder 1.5+tsp black pepper(this is a minimum, honestly you can add as much as you feel) 1tsp paprika(smoked preferred, if you can find both in the pantry use 1stp each) 1tsp coriander(I think I saw this in the spice drawer, but I forget) .5tsp cumin .5tsp cayenne(if want spicy) 1.5tsp parsley 1.5tsp italian seasonings/herbs(don’t worry it won’t taste like italian food) 1tsp oregano .5tsp chili flakes(if want spicy) 1 Bayleaf(optional)

Directions: Cut vegetables: Onions should be in crescents and can be of any size. Larger pieces will better maintain shape where smaller ones will dissolve. Peppers can be cut into strips first and then cut perpendicular to their longest side so you turn the strips into chunks, this can vary in size depending on if you want the peppers to retain shape or dissolve. Cut potatoes and tomatoes into chunks. I suggest not making either particularly small unless you want them to dissolve(I recommend against this, personally I’d keep them at the smallest the size of my thumb) Smash and cut garlic into fine pieces Make seasoning blend listed above. Put chicken, prepped veg and seasoning into pressure cooker(not the beans, if you got them). Then in a cup (or you can make it in the pressure cooker if you’re willing to put a little elbow grease) get a tsp of better than bouillon and a cup of water for each cup of stock you want to add. Stir until paste is mixed in the water and add into pot(if you didn’t make it in the pot) and stir all the components so everything is mixed and coated in seasoning With all the ingredients in the pot: meat, prepared veg, seasoning and stock(not beans). Close pot and turn on pressure cooker for 15-20 minutes. Wait duration of time When done open pot and check for seasoning. If salt is needed I recommend doing it in .5tsp increments with an upper limit of additional 1.5tsp salt. You may add more spices and herbs as you see fit, again suggesting in .5tsp increments. When seasoning is done you may add the beans and stir them gently in so they can sit in the broth. Should be done when that is completed.
